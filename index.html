Work-Style / Situational Judgment Assessments (General)

Applies to any company using this format, not just Google. Based on public candidate reports and general SJT guidance, not any specific vendor's confidential content.

  • What they are: Non-technical screens (Likert-scale statements or scenario + "pick most/least likely action" items) used to profile collaboration style, conscientiousness, and judgment under ambiguity before a recruiter ever talks to you. Google's version is the GHA; other companies run their own versions of the same idea (often built on SHL, Criteria Corp, or similar platforms).
  • Read the whole scenario first. Don't answer off the first sentence. Most SJT scenarios bury the actual constraint (a deadline, a stakeholder conflict, a policy line) two or three sentences in.
  • Align answers to the role, not to abstract virtue. When every option sounds reasonable, ask which one a strong performer in *this specific role* would actually do first. For PM roles that's usually: clarify/align stakeholders before unilateral action, escalate genuine blockers rather than sit on them, and default to data over gut feel when both are available.
  • Consistency beats optimization. These tools cross-check your answers against each other. Answer the same underlying personality honestly across all items rather than trying to game each one individually — the aggregate contradiction is what gets flagged, not any single "imperfect" answer.
  • No prize for speed. Most of these have generous or no time limits. Slow down on scenario-based items specifically; rushed reading is the most common self-inflicted mistake candidates report.
